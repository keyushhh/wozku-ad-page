How is this calculated?

Each row models one activation. We take your audience size, apply the share rate to get the number of people who actually post, then multiply by their average network to get reach. Reach becomes clicks at your click-through rate, and clicks become conversions at your conversion rate.

Wozku spend is charged per person who shares, at $2 each. The comparison cost is what those same conversions would have cost through paid channels, at your cost-per-registration. Savings are the difference, and the multiple is the paid-channel cost divided by the Wozku spend.
