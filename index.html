The questions we get asked first.

Everything worth knowing before you put this on an event.

Will attendees actually post?

Some will not, and that is fine. Nobody is required to take part and non-participants are never tracked or contacted. With a live leaderboard and a prize on the table, participation runs 40 to 70 percent of an event, and higher among the people who reach your booth.

Won't every post look identical?

You can create as many variations per post as you need, each with a different hook, and each graphic may carry the sharer's own name and photo or just a photo. If you want attendees can edit before they post. The feed does not read as automated because it isn't.

How much does my team have to do?

One person, under 15 minutes, before the event. During the event, a wozku page with QR code goes on a screen. That is the operational footprint.

Is this an employee advocacy tool?

No. There is no roster to enrol, no weekly content queue, no dashboard tracking who shared and who didn't. It runs for one event and stops.

Does this work for virtual events and webinars?

Yes. The QR code is for the room. For virtual events the same campaign runs from a link in the registration email, the chat, or the follow-up.

Which networks does it post to?

LinkedIn, Slack and X. Attendees connect their own account once and approve every post. Nothing is ever posted on their behalf.

Do attendees need to install anything?

No. The QR code opens a browser page. There is no app and no account to create.

How far in advance do we need to set this up?

A contest takes under 15 minutes to build. Campaigns that run before, during and after the event get better results, so a week or two of lead time helps, but same-day setup is possible.

How do we know which leads came from this?

Every share carries a tracked link, and every click, form fill and registration is attributed to the attendee who generated it.
